VISIBLE EVIDENCE. The CMA guidance identifies new-customer offers as targeted price reductions and says an invitation to purchase must make explicit which consumers qualify for the offer.

WHY LOW RISK. An introductory or targeted price is less likely to create a universal-price impression when the eligible group is stated at the offer and the price is not presented as available to everyone.

NOT A VIOLATION BECAUSE. A labelled new-customer reduction is not deceptive merely because it differs from another price; the factual gate is whether eligibility and the later or standard economics are obscured.

LIMITATIONS. The local source supplies a regulator-authored principle rather than a merchant screen. It does not prove the later recurring price, offer duration, or renewal terms of any particular checkout.
