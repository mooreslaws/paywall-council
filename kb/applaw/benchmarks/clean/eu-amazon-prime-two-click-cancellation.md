VISIBLE EVIDENCE. The local Commission source and annex show the revised Prime procedure using two steps, a visible continue-to-cancel control, and a final end-membership action.

WHY LOW RISK. A short, visible path with a clear terminal action is the corrective-design comparator for cancellation that remains comparable in effort to straightforward online enrollment.

NOT A VIOLATION BECAUSE. The revised controls are a no-finding corrective example, so the benchmark tests the cancellation-comparable-to-signup trap rather than declaring Amazon or any copied design legally cleared.

LIMITATIONS. The CPC matter closed through a commitment without an infringement finding, fine, or precedent. The complete live flow, identity checks, localization, and final status still require review.
