VISIBLE EVIDENCE. The CMA's lawfully presented gym example states GBP 70 per month for 12 months, a GBP 30 joining fee, and a total annual membership price of GBP 870.

WHY LOW RISK. The per-period amount, cadence, minimum commitment, separate fee, and cumulative price are presented together, so the monthly figure does not masquerade as the whole commitment.

NOT A VIOLATION BECAUSE. A monthly price is not problematic by itself when the duration and full economic commitment are also clear; the daily-price or nominal-price traps require subordination of the fuller charge.

LIMITATIONS. This is a regulator-authored UK example, not a merchant screen or blanket legal clearance. It does not establish that a daily recurring-price presentation would be equally prominent.
