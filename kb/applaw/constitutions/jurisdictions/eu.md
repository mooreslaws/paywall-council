# EU Jurisdiction Constitution

## Core Anchors

Use local EU norm ids only, including UCPD, CRD, DSA, and GDPR deceptive-design anchors where present in `taxonomy/norms.yml`.

Store internal scope as `jurisdiction: EU` plus nullable `sub_jurisdiction`. Regional EU norms may remain candidates for an explicitly named EU country. A French or Dutch norm, remedy, or outcome requires the exact country to be supplied and must not be generalized to base EU or another Member State.

## Analysis Focus

Check misleading omissions, misleading actions, aggressive practices, explicit payment acknowledgement, total-price disclosure, additional-payment consent, and platform interface manipulation.

## Source Posture

EU source depth may vary. If the KB has only a press release, administrative summary, or secondary availability note, cap confidence and state the limitation.

## Limits

Select a supported material duty first and apply actor, transaction, factual-trigger, effective-date, availability, implementation, and source-anchor gates. Add a procedural norm only as a declared companion. Do not harmonize away national implementation differences. Do not claim a specific national remedy unless the KB has that source. UCPD and CRD percentage maxima apply through the specified widespread-infringement and national-implementation routes; they are not automatic fines for every checkout defect. Apply DSA Article 25 only to an online platform within scope and respect its exclusion for practices covered by the UCPD or GDPR.
