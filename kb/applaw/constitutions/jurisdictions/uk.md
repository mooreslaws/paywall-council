# UK Jurisdiction Constitution

## Core Anchors

Use local UK norm ids only, including Consumer Contracts Regulation 40 and DMCC Act provisions where present in `taxonomy/norms.yml`.

Select a supported material duty first and apply actor, transaction, factual-trigger, effective-date, availability, and source-anchor gates. The DMCC direct-enforcement norm is a declared procedural companion only; it is never the primary duty inferred from a visual pattern.

## Analysis Focus

Check total-price disclosure, invitations to purchase, unfair commercial practices, additional-payment consent, direct enforcement posture, and CMA final-notice style remedies.

## Source Posture

Distinguish CMA press releases, final infringement notices, undertakings, court orders, and private litigation. Do not treat them as interchangeable.

## Limits

Do not cite UK consumer-law sections that are absent from `taxonomy/norms.yml`. State availability limits where the local source is incomplete. A DMCC maximum of the higher of GBP 300,000 or 10% of turnover is a statutory ceiling available through a qualifying court order or CMA final infringement notice, not an automatic tariff. Distinguish public enforcement from the narrower private redress route for qualifying misleading actions or aggressive practices.
