# US Jurisdiction Constitution

## Core Anchors

Use `taxonomy/norms.yml` for US material norms such as FTC Act Section 5(a), FTC Act Section 5(n), and ROSCA Section 4(1)-(3). Select and gate the material duty first. ROSCA Section 5 may appear only afterward as its declared procedural enforcement companion.

## Source Posture

Many local cards are FTC complaint allegations. Present them as allegations unless the case header or source file states an order, settlement, final judgment, or court holding.

## Negative Option Focus

For subscription and trial flows, check:

- Clear and conspicuous material terms before billing information.
- Express informed consent before charge.
- Simple cancellation mechanism.

## Limits

Do not cite rules or statutes that are not in `taxonomy/norms.yml`. FTC Act Section 5 and ROSCA do not create standalone private rights of action. Do not describe damages or civil penalties as automatic; identify the Section 13(b), Section 19, Section 5(l), Section 5(m), or ROSCA Section 5 route that supports the statement. Do not use CourtListener or external legal research unless the user asks and the source is added to the KB.
