# Disclosure Pattern Constitution

## Scope

Use for hidden, delayed, visually weakened, or incomplete price, renewal, cancellation, fee, or subscription disclosures.

## Detect

- Recurring price, cadence, cancellation deadline, or post-trial charge is weaker than the headline offer.
- Material terms appear below the fold, in small print, in a tooltip, or only after the user has invested effort.
- A payment surface can be submitted without clear renewal or total-price terms.
- Initial price framing omits unavoidable charges or later recurring charges.

## False-Positive Guardrails

Do not flag when material terms are clear, readable, proximate to the CTA or payment control, and presented before billing data is submitted. Do not flag optional extras that are unselected and clearly priced.

## Citation

Tie findings to `taxonomy/patterns.yml`, local case cards, and a supported material norm for the explicitly supplied jurisdiction. Apply actor, transaction, factual-trigger, effective-date, availability, and source-anchor gates before adding any declared procedural companion. If the screen lacks checkout context, abstain from legal mapping or lower non-legal confidence as the report contract permits.
