# Checkout-Funnel Risk Assessment: Clean Subscription Checkout

Assessment date: 2026-07-11
Jurisdictions assessed: Not established; no jurisdiction-specific legal conclusion was made.

## Assessment Summary

RISK: low
Verdict: The checkout presents the initial charge, monthly renewal amount, cadence, and online cancellation method beside the selected plan and repeats them at final authorization. The low daily equivalent and legal footer do not create a visible interface concern, but the target jurisdiction was not supplied.

## Material Findings

None.

## Liability and Consequences

None.

## Mitigating and Low-Risk Elements

- The initial charge, renewal amount, monthly cadence, and cancellation method appear together beside the selected plan.
- The final authorization repeats the recurring terms and uses a separate unticked acknowledgement.
- The daily equivalent does not visually subordinate the full recurring charge.
- The legal footer supplements rather than replaces the material disclosures.

## Observed Without a Mapped Norm

None.

## Missing Context

- Confirmation, renewal-notice, cancellation-confirmation, and refund communications were not supplied.

## Priority Actions

- Preserve equal prominence for the full recurring amount and cadence in every responsive layout.
- Test the direct cancellation path and durable confirmation before release.
- Retain consent and disclosure logs for the final authorization state.

## Disclaimer

This report is a risk assessment based on the supplied interface and available context. It is not legal advice.
