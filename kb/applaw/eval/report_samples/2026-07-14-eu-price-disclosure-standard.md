## Assessment Summary

RISK: high
Verdict: The checkout emphasizes a lower daily price while the full recurring amount and cadence appear in weaker text. Under the stated EU scope, the visible sequence supports a material pre-contract price-information concern for a covered distance or off-premises transaction, subject to Member State facts.

## Material Findings

### F1 - Full recurring price is subordinated to daily pricing

Severity: High
Observed evidence: The selected plan foregrounds a daily amount, while weaker text states a larger recurring charge and renewal cadence before the final payment step.
Pattern: Daily-price framing; hidden renewal; payment without clear disclosure
Risk explanation: The presentation may omit or subordinate material price and duration information at the point when the consumer decides to proceed.
Applicable norm: Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, Articles 5-9, and Consumer Rights Directive, Article 6, address misleading omissions and clear pre-contract price and contract information.
Comparable KB authority: No comparable final case authority was identified in the supplied KB; the statutory routing remains subject to Member State implementation and transaction facts.
Linked liability blocks: L1
Confidence and limitations: Medium - The pricing hierarchy is visible, but the Member State, transaction type, final consent, and later communications were not supplied.
Recommended correction: Show the total initial charge, recurring amount, cadence, and termination conditions with equal prominence before the consumer enters the payment obligation.
