# Checkout-Funnel Risk Assessment: UK Price Disclosure Fixture

Assessment date: 2026-07-14
Jurisdictions assessed: United Kingdom (UK statutory analysis; territorial and consumer-scope facts require confirmation).

## Assessment Summary

RISK: high
Verdict: The checkout presents a package price before the mandatory booking fee is included, then reveals the higher total later in the payment journey. Under the stated UK scope, the visible sequence supports a material invitation-to-purchase price-omission concern.

## Material Findings

### F1 - Mandatory fee is omitted from the initial package price

Severity: High
Observed evidence: The package selector shows lesson prices while the mandatory booking fee is not included or explained until the later payment total.
Pattern: Mandatory fee drip pricing; payment without clear disclosure
Risk explanation: The first invitation to purchase does not show the total price even though the additional fee is reasonably calculable before the consumer proceeds.
Applicable norm: Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, sections 225 and 230, address unfair practices and omissions of material price information in an invitation to purchase.
Comparable KB authority: CMA Final Infringement Notice against Automobile Association Developments Limited, a settled final notice, concerned mandatory booking fees omitted at earlier invitations to purchase.
Linked liability blocks: L1
Confidence and limitations: High - The omission and later total are visible, but the trader's full offer rules and territorial facts were not supplied.
Recommended correction: Include the mandatory fee in the first total price shown at the invitation to purchase and repeat the complete total beside the final payment action.
